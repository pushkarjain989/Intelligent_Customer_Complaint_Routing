Introduction & Problem Statement
The Challenge in Customer Support Triage
Why traditional automation tools and generative AI bots fail in real-world customer operations.
1. High Volume & Delays
Support desks receive tens of thousands of messages across Twitter, email, and web chat.
  • Agents waste 70% of time answering repetitive questions.
  • High response times cause customer frustration and brand damage.
2. Rigid Keyword Rule Systems
Traditional rule engines match strict keywords (e.g. "delay", "baggage").
  • Cannot understand synonyms, intent, or conversational context.
  • Requires endless manual rule maintenance whenever topics change.
3. Unchecked LLM Hallucinations
Raw Generative LLMs lack deterministic safety guardrails.
  • May hallucinate non-existent company compensation policies.
  • Risk sending cheerful, automated replies to deeply distressed customers.
🎯 Project Objective:
Build an intelligent, unsupervised NLP triage engine that automatically groups complaints, flags hostile customers, catches bizarre anomalies, and reliably decides whether a ticket is safe for AI auto-reply or requires immediate human specialist escalation.
Proposed Solution
AutoRoute AI: Safe, Adaptive Decision Engine
Combining unsupervised machine learning with multi-layer safety guardrails.
Unsupervised Machine Learning
Zero-Annotation Semantic Clustering
Uses Sentence-Transformers and KMeans to discover natural complaint topics automatically without requiring expensive pre-labeled training datasets. Automatically names each department using TF-IDF term salience.
Safety Layer 1
Sentiment Outrage Gating
Real-time VADER sentiment scoring monitors customer hostility. If compound polarity is severely negative (score ≤ -0.50), automated replies are strictly bypassed and the ticket is escalated to priority human supervisors.
Safety Layer 2
Dual Anomaly Detection
Combines DBSCAN spatial density and Isolation Forest to catch out-of-domain vocabulary, gibberish, and novel complaint edge-cases. Both models must agree a ticket is safe before AI is permitted to reply.
Dynamic Adaptation
100% Dataset Agnostic
The platform is not locked to one industry. Uploading an Airline dataset, a Telecom dataset, or a Banking dataset immediately re-configures all clusters, benchmark presets, 2D visual maps, and policy templates dynamically.

Methodology: Part 1
NLP Preprocessing & Semantic Representation
Transforming raw unstructured text into mathematical representations of meaning.
Text Preprocessing
spaCy Normalization
  • Noise Filtering: Strips handles (@user), URLs, and non-alphanumeric symbols.
  • Lemmatization: Reduces inflectional variations to root forms (e.g., "cancelled" → "cancel").
  • Stopwords: Filters uninformative vocabulary to enhance model focus.
Dense Vector Embedding
all-MiniLM-L6-v2
  • Maps variable-length sentences into a fixed 384-dimensional vector space.
  • Unlike keyword matching, sentences with similar meanings map close together.
  • Ultra-fast inference (sub-15ms on CPU).
Similarity Computation
L2-Norm & Cosine Metric
  • Every embedding vector is scaled to unit length (||v|| = 1.0).
  • Cosine similarity between query and cluster centroids reduces to a direct dot product:
    Sim(u,v) = u • v
TF-IDF Cluster Naming: Automatically fits a TF-IDF N-gram model on cluster members to discover the top 8 distinctive terms (e.g. "delay", "gate", "hours") and generates clean, human-readable category labels.
Methodology: Part 2
Unsupervised Clustering & Triple Safety Layer
Mathematical clustering paired with density and emotional safeguards.
Clustering Engine
K-Means (k=6)
Partitions the 384-dimensional semantic space into 6 core operational topic clusters by minimizing within-cluster inertia.
  • Stores cluster centroids.
  • Preserves member exemplars for nearest-neighbor fallback matching.
Dual Anomaly Engine
DBSCAN + Isolation Forest
Two complementary models to catch outliers:
  • DBSCAN: Flags queries exceeding radius ε = 0.48 from core points.
  • Isolation Forest: 100 decision trees isolating anomalous feature depths.
Emotional Gating
VADER Sentiment Outrage
Rule-based sentiment analyzer tuned for social capitalization and punctuation.
  • Compound score ∈ [-1.0, +1.0].
  • Scores ≤ -0.50 trigger instant priority escalation to human managers.
2D Dimensionality Reduction (PCA): Projects the 384-dimensional embeddings into 2 principal components (PC1, PC2) to render live cluster boundaries, centroids, and real-time query positions on an interactive scatter map.
Methodology: Part 3
Decision Rules & Contextual Policy RAG
Deterministic decision matrix and structured, policy-compliant response generation.
Decision Engine Rule Hierarchy
ConditionOutcomeAction
Compound ≤ -0.50 HUMAN Senior Supervisor Priority Queue
DBSCAN / IsoForest Outlier HUMAN Technical Specialist Triage
Similarity ≥ Threshold AI REPLY Autonomous Empathetic Resolution
Similarity < Threshold HUMAN Standard Agent Review Desk
Contextual Policy RAG (Response Generation)
  • Entity Extraction: Mines operational metadata (Flight/Ticket numbers, PNR codes, monetary amounts, durations).
  • Regulatory Policy Retrieval: Dynamically injects matching corporate SLAs (e.g. Flight Delay Compensation, Baggage Liability, Software Bug SLAs).
  • Empathetic Synthesis: Generates structured, polite resolution emails tailored to the exact category.
